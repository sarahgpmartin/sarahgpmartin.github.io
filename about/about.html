ABOUT SARAH MARTIN

WEB DESIGNER & WEB DEVELOPER

My name is Sarah Martin, and I am a Digital Designer with experience in web design and application design. I am adaptable, organized, detail-oriented, and skilled in problem-solving with strong communication skills. I work with HTML, CSS, and industry-standard design tools. I am passionate about improving and creating user-friendly experiences.

I am currently pursuing a Bachelor of Arts in Digital Design with an emphasis in Web Design at Grand Canyon University. As a Christian, I strive to reflect my faith through my creativity and through my actions.

Outside of work and school, you can usually find me enjoying a good cup of coffee or boba. I also love spending time with friends and family, whether that means hanging out, talking, or simply enjoying each other's company!
